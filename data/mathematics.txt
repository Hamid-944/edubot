MATHEMATICS – School Study Material

=== NUMBER SYSTEMS ===
Natural numbers are the counting numbers we use every day: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and so on. They do not include zero or negative numbers. Whole numbers include all natural numbers plus zero: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Integers include all whole numbers and their negative counterparts: ..., -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ...

Rational numbers are numbers that can be written as a fraction p/q, where p and q are integers and q is not zero. Examples include 1/2, 3/4, -5/3, and even whole numbers like 4 (which equals 4/1). Irrational numbers cannot be written as a simple fraction. Pi (π ≈ 3.14159...) and the square root of 2 (√2 ≈ 1.41421...) are irrational numbers. Real numbers include both rational and irrational numbers.

=== FRACTIONS AND DECIMALS ===
A fraction represents a part of a whole. In the fraction 3/4, the number on top (3) is the numerator and the number on bottom (4) is the denominator. This means we have 3 parts out of 4 equal parts.

To add fractions with the same denominator, simply add the numerators: 1/5 + 2/5 = 3/5. To add fractions with different denominators, first find the Least Common Denominator (LCD). For 1/3 + 1/4, the LCD is 12, so we get 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12.

To multiply fractions, multiply the numerators together and the denominators together: 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2. To divide fractions, flip the second fraction (find its reciprocal) and then multiply: 2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 2/3 × 5/4 = 10/12 = 5/6.

Decimals are another way to show parts of a whole. The decimal 0.75 is the same as 75/100 or 3/4. To convert a fraction to a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator: 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75.

=== PERCENTAGES ===
A percentage is a fraction out of 100. The word "percent" means "per hundred." So 25% means 25 out of every 100, which equals 25/100 or 0.25.

To find a percentage of a number, convert the percentage to a decimal and multiply. For example, 20% of 50 = 0.20 × 50 = 10. To find what percentage one number is of another, divide the first number by the second and multiply by 100. For example, 15 is what percent of 60? Answer: (15 ÷ 60) × 100 = 25%.

Percentage increase = (New Value - Old Value) / Old Value × 100. If a price goes from $40 to $50, the percentage increase = (50 - 40) / 40 × 100 = 25%.

=== BASIC ALGEBRA ===
Algebra uses letters (variables) to represent unknown numbers. The variable x in the equation x + 5 = 12 stands for an unknown value. To solve for x, subtract 5 from both sides: x = 12 - 5 = 7.

An expression is a combination of numbers, variables, and operations without an equals sign. Examples: 3x + 2, 5y - 7, 2a + 3b. An equation has an equals sign and states that two expressions are equal, like 2x + 3 = 11.

To solve a linear equation, use inverse operations to isolate the variable. For 2x + 3 = 11: subtract 3 from both sides to get 2x = 8, then divide both sides by 2 to get x = 4.

The distributive property states that a(b + c) = ab + ac. For example, 3(x + 4) = 3x + 12. Like terms are terms with the same variable and exponent. You can add like terms: 3x + 5x = 8x, but you cannot add unlike terms like 3x + 5y.

=== GEOMETRY ===
A point is a location in space with no size. A line is a straight path that extends infinitely in both directions. A ray starts at a point and extends infinitely in one direction. A line segment is a part of a line with two endpoints.

An angle is formed by two rays sharing a common endpoint (vertex). Angles are measured in degrees. A right angle measures exactly 90°. An acute angle measures less than 90°. An obtuse angle measures more than 90° but less than 180°. A straight angle measures exactly 180°.

A triangle is a polygon with three sides and three angles. The sum of all interior angles in a triangle is always 180°. In a right triangle, one angle is 90°. The longest side opposite the right angle is called the hypotenuse. According to the Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse.

The perimeter of a shape is the total distance around its outside edge. To find the perimeter of a rectangle: P = 2 × (length + width). For a square with side s: P = 4s.

The area of a shape is the amount of surface it covers. Area of a rectangle = length × width. Area of a triangle = (1/2) × base × height. Area of a circle = π × r², where r is the radius.

The volume of a 3D shape is the amount of space it occupies. Volume of a cube = side³. Volume of a rectangular prism = length × width × height. Volume of a cylinder = π × r² × height.

=== STATISTICS AND PROBABILITY ===
The mean (average) of a data set is found by adding all values and dividing by the count. For the data set {4, 7, 2, 9, 3}, the mean = (4 + 7 + 2 + 9 + 3) ÷ 5 = 25 ÷ 5 = 5.

The median is the middle value when data is arranged in order. For {2, 3, 4, 7, 9}, the median is 4. If there is an even number of values, the median is the average of the two middle values.

The mode is the value that appears most often in a data set. For {3, 5, 5, 7, 9, 5}, the mode is 5 because it appears three times.

The range is the difference between the highest and lowest values: Range = Maximum - Minimum. For {2, 3, 4, 7, 9}, the range = 9 - 2 = 7.

Probability measures how likely an event is to occur. It is expressed as a number between 0 and 1. Probability of an event = Number of favorable outcomes ÷ Total number of outcomes. The probability of rolling a 3 on a fair six-sided die = 1/6.

=== ORDER OF OPERATIONS ===
When solving a mathematical expression with multiple operations, follow PEMDAS: Parentheses first, then Exponents, then Multiplication and Division (left to right), then Addition and Subtraction (left to right).

For example, 3 + 4 × 2 = 3 + 8 = 11 (not 14, because multiplication comes before addition). Another example: (3 + 4) × 2 = 7 × 2 = 14 (parentheses come first).
